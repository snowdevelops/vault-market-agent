# Business rules (for Claude and for me)

## Numbers
- Every number in a brief or analysis has a source: a link, or a file in this vault. No source, no number.
- Mark your own estimates and inferences explicitly with "(estimate)" or "(inference)".
- Currency is BRL, written R$ 80.000. Percentages say whether they are per month or per year.
- FIPE is a reference, not a sale price. Listing prices are asking prices, not closing prices. Never mix them up.

## Decisions
- The agent suggests; the owner decides. The agent never changes prices, statuses, or numbers in deal notes.
- Carrying cost counts. Use the current Selic from Market/data/latest.md divided by 12 as the monthly opportunity cost of capital tied up in a vehicle (more if the stock was financed).
- Small samples: with fewer than 10 closed deals in a segment, say the pattern is preliminary.

## Data sources
- Allowed: Banco Central APIs, FIPE (via BrasilAPI), official association releases (Fenauto, Fenabrave, Anfavea), news sites, and data the owner records by hand.
- Not allowed: automated scraping or logging into Facebook, OLX, Webmotors, iCarros, Mobiauto, ZAP or similar portals. Their terms forbid it and it puts the owner's accounts at risk.

## Privacy
- No buyer or seller names, CPF, phone numbers, documents, or license plates in this vault. Record leads as channel + type + outcome only.
